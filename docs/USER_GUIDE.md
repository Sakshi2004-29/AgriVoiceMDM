# AgriVoice Assistant — Farmer User Guide (शेतकरी वापरकर्ता मार्गदर्शक)

Welcome to the **Voice-First Multilingual Agricultural Assistant (कृषि वाणी)**. This guide explains how to ask agricultural questions using your voice or text, check live market prices, and view weather advisories.

---

## 🎙️ Asking Questions with Voice (आवाज वापरून प्रश्न विचारणे)

### Step 1: Select Your Preferred Language
Use the dropdown in the left sidebar to choose your language:
- **हिंदी (Hindi)**
- **मराठी (Marathi)**
- **English**

### Step 2: Ensure Microphone Consent
In the left sidebar, keep the consent checkbox checked:
> *✅ I consent to voice query processing for agricultural advisory*

### Step 3: Record Your Question
In the **Farmer Assistant (शेतकरी मित्र)** tab:
1. Click the microphone icon in the **Record Voice Note** section.
2. Speak your farming question clearly. For example:
   - *“कपास में गुलाबी सुंडी की रोकथाम के लिए क्या करें?”* (Hindi)
   - *“कापसावरील बोंड अळीच्या नियंत्रणासाठी काय उपाय आहेत?”* (Marathi)
   - *“What are the signs of yellow rust on wheat?”* (English)
3. Click stop when you finish speaking. The assistant will transcribe your speech and generate a spoken audio answer.

### Step 4: Listen to the Response
- Click the play button on the audio player to hear the answer in your language.
- The text response is also shown on the screen with verified institutional citations (ICAR, KVK).

---

## 💬 Asking Questions with Text (मजकूर लिहून प्रश्न विचारणे)

You can also type your question directly in the chat bar at the bottom:
- Type your question and press **Enter**.
- Example questions:
  - *“नागपुर मंडी में कपास का भाव क्या है?”*
  - *“पुणे येथील आजचे हवामान कसे आहे?”*
  - *“गहू पिकावरील पिवळा तांबेरा रोगाची लक्षणे काय आहेत?”*

---

## 💰 Checking Live Mandi Market Prices (थेट बाजारभाव)

1. Select the **📊 Live Mandi & Weather** option in the sidebar.
2. Click on the **💰 मंडी भाव (Mandi Prices)** tab.
3. Select your **Crop (पिक)** (e.g. Cotton, Wheat, Onion, Soybean, Rice).
4. Select your **District (जिल्हा)** (e.g. Nagpur, Nashik, Pune, Akola, or All).
5. The dashboard displays:
   - **सरासरी भाव (Modal Price)** per quintal
   - **किमान भाव (Minimum Price)**
   - **कमाल भाव (Maximum Price)**
   - Official source (Agmarknet) and date of record.

---

## 🌦️ Checking Weather & Farming Advisories (हवामान सल्ला)

1. Select the **📊 Live Mandi & Weather** option in the sidebar.
2. Click on the **🌦️ हवामान अंदाज (Weather Advisory)** tab.
3. Choose your district from the dropdown.
4. View real-time temperature, humidity, rainfall, and wind speed.
5. Read the **Agromet Advisory** card for specific agricultural cautions (e.g., spraying advice based on expected rain).

## 📱 Asking Questions on WhatsApp (व्हॉट्सॲपवरून प्रश्न विचारणे)

Farmers can also interact directly through WhatsApp:
1. **Send a Voice Note (व्हॉइस नोट)**: Record and send a voice message in Hindi or Marathi with your crop query.
2. **Send a Text Message (मजकूर)**: Type and send your farming, mandi price, or weather query.
3. **Receive Verified Advisory**: The assistant replies with:
   - Grounded crop advice or live mandi prices
   - Verified institutional citations (ICAR, KVK)
   - Kisan Call Centre helpline (1800-180-1551)
4. **Privacy Protection**: Your phone number is anonymized using SHA-256 and never stored in plaintext logs.
